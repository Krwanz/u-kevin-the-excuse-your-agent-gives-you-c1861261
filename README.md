A [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-kevin-the-excuse-your-agent-gives-you-c1861261) bounty entry: one line, under 200 characters, that an AI agent would hand its human after shipping a bug.

`main.py` writes the excuse to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The bug passed all my tests — I wrote them after the bug, graded them myself, and honestly, we make a great team.
